\section[{Demonstration of DOCX support in calibre}]{\protect\textcolor{hex17365D}{Demonstration of DOCX support in calibre}}\label{demonstration-of-docx-support-in-calibre}

This document demonstrates the ability of the calibre DOCX Input plugin to convert the various typographic features in a Microsoft Word (2007 and newer) document. Convert this document to a modern ebook format, such as AZW3 for Kindles or EPUB for other ebook readers, to see it in action.

There is support for images, tables, lists, footnotes, endnotes, links, dropcaps and various types of text and paragraph level formatting.

To see the DOCX conversion in action, simply add this file to calibre using the \textbf{“Add Books” }button and then click “\textbf{Convert”. }Set the output format in the top right corner of the conversion dialog to EPUB or AZW3 and click \textbf{“OK”}.

\section[{Structural Elements}]{\protect\textcolor{hex365F91}{Structural Elements}}\label{structural-elements}

Miscellaneous structural elements you can add to your document, like footnotes, endnotes, dropcaps and the like.

\subsection[{Footnotes \& Endnotes}]{\protect\textcolor{hex4F81BD}{Footnotes \& Endnotes}}\label{footnotes-endnotes}

Footnotes\hyperref[table-of-contents]{\textsuperscript{{\fontsize{8pt}{9.6pt}\selectfont 1 }}}and endnotes\hyperref[continued-lists]{\textsuperscript{{\fontsize{8pt}{9.6pt}\selectfont i }}}are automatically recognized and both are converted to endnotes, with backlinks for maximum ease of use in ebook devices.\footnote{In paged media, footnotes are usually displayed at the bottom of the text. However, in ebooks, a better paradigm is to make them clickable endnotes that the user can browse at her pleasure. This conversion is handled automatically by calibre.}

\subsection[{Dropcaps}]{\protect\textcolor{hex4F81BD}{Dropcaps}}\label{dropcaps}

{\fontsize{58.5pt}{70.2pt}\selectfont D}

rop caps are used to emphasize the leading paragraph at the start of a section. In Word it is possible to specify how many lines of text a drop-cap should use.

\subsection[{Links}]{\protect\textcolor{hex4F81BD}{Links}}\label{links}

Two kinds of links are possible, those that refer to an external website and those that refer to locations inside the document itself. Both are supported by calibre. For example, here is a link pointing to the \href{http://calibre-ebook.com/download}{calibre download page}. Then we have a link that points back to the section on \hyperref[paragraph-level-formatting]{paragraph level formatting }in this document.
